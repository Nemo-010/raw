## What we believed

That DeepInfra offered a free tier whose size had not been read, recorded at the
`unverified` rung with a `qualifies-with-caveat` verdict.

## What we found

The pricing page states the opposite in one sentence: "You have to add a card or pre-pay or
you won't be able to use our services. Pre-paid accounts draw down their balance as they
go."

The only free balance on the site is a promotion, "we're thrilled to offer you $20 in free
credits to explore DeepInfra's AI inference cloud", claimed through a deal link aimed at
viewers of one YouTube channel. A channel promotion is not a free tier.

## What it changes

The verdict becomes `paid-only` and the rung becomes `documented`, because a page was read.
A second `unverified` record that asserted a free tier without reading one is now a
documented record that refutes it, which is the direction the ladder is supposed to move.

## The transferable point

`unverified` is a holding state, not an answer. A record that says "reported, not read"
should be read at the next opportunity, because the reported claim is as likely to be wrong
as right, and this one was wrong in the direction that matters: it made a prepaid service
look reachable with no payment instrument.
