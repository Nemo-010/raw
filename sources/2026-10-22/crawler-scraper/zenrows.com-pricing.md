1. How is pricing calculated? +

   You spend credits, not dollars, so you only pay for the work you actually run. One credit is a standard successful page. Harder work draws more from the same balance: JavaScript rendering costs 5 credits, premium proxies 10, and both together 25. Residential bandwidth draws a flat 25,000 credits per GB (that's 1,000 hard-protected pages), and Browser bills bandwidth plus a flat 5 credits per minute of session time. Every plan runs off one credit balance.
2. Do you charge for failed requests? +

   Never. You only spend credits on successful requests. Not Found (404) and Gone (410) responses count as successful results.
3. Do you offer discounts? +

   Yes. Paying yearly gives you two months free (pay for 10, get 12) and the full year's credit allowance up front. Enterprise plans are priced on committed volume.
4. What happens if I hit my monthly limit? +

   We warn you before you reach it. On a paid plan you can add extra credits (top-ups) any time to keep running, up to ten times per billing period, and unused top-up credits roll into the next billing period once, then expire. On Free there are no top-ups: you wait for the next monthly refresh or upgrade to a paid plan. Either way, no lost data or configuration.
5. Can I upgrade or downgrade at any time? +

   Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately, with billing adjusted accordingly. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing cycle. No penalties, no hidden fees.
6. What are concurrent requests? +

   Concurrency is how many requests run at the same time. The limit scales with your plan, so larger plans run more jobs in parallel.
7. How does protected access work? +

   Same primitives, same API. Set the protection level and Zenrows routes through the right stack (residential network, fingerprinting, retries, the Recovery Engine) so your code doesn't change between an open page and a hard-protected one.

   Same toolkit, same workflow. Set the protection level and Zenrows picks the right route in the background. Your team's workflow looks the same whether the source is open or hard-protected.
8. Does Zenrows work with Claude, Cursor, and Codex? +

   Yes. The Agent Toolkit ships an MCP server, a CLI, and SDKs that snap into Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Windsurf, Gemini CLI, and any MCP-compatible client. Every primitive is exposed as a tool, so agents call them natively with auth built in.

   Yes. The Agent Toolkit plugs into Claude, Cursor, Codex, Windsurf, Gemini, and other AI assistants your team uses. Every primitive is a ready-made tool, with permissions and activity logs included.
9. Do you offer SLAs and enterprise security? +

   Yes, on Enterprise. An SLA, SOC 2 and ISO 27001 compliance, dedicated account management, premium support, and quarterly business reviews (QBRs).
