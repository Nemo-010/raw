## Pricing at a glance

Credit unit

1 credit = 1 page on a basic scrape, crawl, or map. Search costs 2 credits per 10 results; Interact costs 2 credits per browser minute. On Scrape and Crawl, the JSON, Question, and Highlight formats add 4 credits per page; Monitor's deterministic-extraction engine bills a flat 7 credits per page per check instead. Credits are shared across all endpoints: search results and pages scraped draw on one balance.

Free tier

1,000 credits every month, refreshed monthly, no credit card required.

Entry plan

Hobby: $19/month billed monthly, or $16/month billed annually, for 5,000 credits per month (2,500 searches or 5,000 pages scraped).

Failed requests

A scrape that returns no result is not charged. A page that responds with an error status such as 403 or 404 is still returned to you and costs 1 credit.

Effective September 4, 2026. Prices in USD.

You set a monthly limit to cap the spend, and you can buy credits yourself at any time.

Credits roll over on Scale and Enterprise. Scale carries unused credits for one month, and Enterprise carries a custom amount. Hobby, Standard and Growth credits do not roll over.

Credits you buy stay on your account until you use them. They expire if you cancel your subscription, so use them before you leave.
