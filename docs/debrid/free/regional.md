# Regional markets

The English comparison sites list nine services. The other markets sell the same
job under other names, at other prices, behind other payment rails. This document
records what each market has that the Anglophone list does not.

## The answer

⭐ **Two regional offers beat every Western price.** 115's traffic pack at CNY
38.88/TB is the cheapest retrieval in the corpus, and Debrid Italia's EUR 21.90
year works out to EUR 1.83 a month. Neither is reachable without a local gate: 115
needs a +86 number and Debrid Italia sells through an Italian WhatsApp chat.

⛔ **The gate is the payment rail, not the country.** Two Vietnamese resellers of
the same upstream prove it on one night: LinksVIP takes an international Visa or
MasterCard, while Getlink.vn settles only by bank transfer or a QR for 52 banks,
Momo, ZaloPay and Viettel Money.

⭐ **The Polish market is invisible to English sites.** TwojLimit, Pobierz.biz,
RapidTraffic.pl, NoPremium and Rapideo all price per gigabyte, and only `debrid.pl`,
a Polish calculator, indexes them. Pay-per-GB at EUR 73/TB costs 26 times a monthly
subscription at 1 TB and 390 times at 15 TB, so the model only wins below about
40 GB.

⚠ **"Debrid-France" does not exist.** No search hit, no archive capture for any
TLD, broker 530 twice. It is a list name, not a provider.

## Middle East and Africa

⛔ **Outside Iran there is no native multi-hoster in this region.** Egypt, Nigeria,
Kenya, South Africa, the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Israel all retrieve through a global
service, and the barrier is payment.

⭐ **Iran has three priced native services.** ParsGet publishes 3 GB a day free and
100, 300 or 600 GB a month at 348,000, 748,000 and 1,398,000 Toman, with WebDAV from
the Basic tier. RapidPars adds private-tracker search and states "online payment through the Shaparak gateway". MultiShare bills per downloaded byte with host coefficients.

⚠ **TorBox is the most reachable global service from these markets**: 20
cryptocurrencies "available in all countries", Patreon, ACH, eGifts24 ZAR gift
cards, mailed cash, but it refuses prepaid cards, which is exactly the instrument
a Saudi buyer was told to use in a 2022 forum thread.

## How to read a regional row

1. Read the local price, then the rail. A price with no reachable rail is a zero.
2. Check the identity gate: `country-phone` is a wall, `email` is not.
3. Convert only with a named rate and date, and keep the local price as stated.
4. Treat a reseller's "official" banner as the shop's claim; the vendor's reseller
 list is usually behind a login.
