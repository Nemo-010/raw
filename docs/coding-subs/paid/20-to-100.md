# The mid ladder, where the paid tiers stop being a fraction of a free tier

Between USD 30 and USD 100 a month the plans stop being samples. A USD 40 plan gives
twenty times a free tier's allowance rather than five, and the constraint that binds
changes from "the allowance is small" to "the allowance is unstated". Every plan in
this bracket states its price and its tier count and publishes no absolute capacity,
so the bracket can be sorted by price and by the shape of its quota, and not by
capacity.

The answer: **OpenCode Go Plus at USD 40 is the only plan here whose ceiling is a
published number.** It carries the same per-model token prices as Go and multiplies
every monthly ceiling, for example GLM-5.3-Flash from USD 60 to USD 180 and
DeepSeek V4.1 Flash from USD 80 to USD 160 (`documented`, 2026-10-22).

## OpenCode Go Plus

Go Plus is USD 40 a month and applies the same token prices as Go to every model,
with a higher monthly ceiling on each (`documented`, `opencode.ai/docs/go`, read
2026-10-22). The ceilings are USD 180 for GLM-5.3-Flash, GLM-5.2 and Kimi K2.7 Code;
USD 120 for GLM-5.3, MiMo-V2.6-Flash, MiMo-V2.5, Muse Spark 1.3 and 1.2 Contributor;
USD 240 for LongCat-2.0; USD 160 for DeepSeek V4.1 Flash; USD 90 for Qwen3.8 Flash;
USD 60 for Kimi K3, Qwen3.8 Max, DeepSeek V4 Pro, Step 5 Preview, Grok 4.7 and the
two MiMo Pro models.

Because the token prices do not change, the multiplier is not a discount and it is
not a bundle. It is a higher balance at the same rate. **The multiple is not uniform:**
across the 33 models that carry a dollar ceiling on both plans, 19 rise by 4x, 7 by 3x,
6 by 2x and 1 by 8x. GLM-5.3 is the 8x row, from USD 15 to USD 120. For a reader who has
already decided the model, the offer is still the clearest in the market: pick the model,
read its ceiling, compare it against the dollar price of the same tokens on the vendor's
own API. ⛔ **Do not assume USD 40 buys four times the Go ceiling**, because on 14 of the
33 models it buys two or three times.

The published request estimates scale with the higher ceiling, so GLM-5.3 on Go Plus
moves from an estimated 1,080 requests a month to 8,640. A reader should read those as
estimates and not as entitlements: they follow from an assumed 700 input, 52,000 cached
and 150 output token shape, and the page states no per-request limit for either plan.

Evidence: `opencode.ai/docs/go` (read 2026-10-22): the Go Plus table with every
per-model ceiling. Verdict: a higher balance at an unchanged rate, by a multiple that
runs from 2x to 8x depending on the model.

## Copilot Pro+

Copilot Pro+ is USD 39 a user a month for USD 70 of monthly AI Credits, four times
the Pro allowance or more, access to premium models including Opus, and audit logs
(`documented`, `github.com/features/copilot/plans`, read 2026-10-22).

The credit allowance nearly doubles per dollar against Pro: USD 1.50 of credits per
dollar on Pro against USD 1.79 on Pro+. That is a mild volume discount and the only
place in this ladder where the ratio improves.

Evidence: `github.com/features/copilot/plans` (read 2026-10-22): Pro+ is USD 39 with
USD 70 of credits and 4x the included usage. Verdict: the same product with a
slightly better credit rate.

## Trae Pro+

Trae Pro+ is USD 60 a month for USD 60 of usage, unlimited autocomplete and up to
fifteen concurrent cloud tasks (`documented`, `www.trae.ai/pricing`, read 2026-10-22).

There is no discount at USD 60 either. The only thing Pro+ adds over Pro is five
more concurrent cloud tasks and three times the balance, at three times the price.

Evidence: `www.trae.ai/pricing` (read 2026-10-22): USD 60 of usage at USD 60. Verdict:
a larger prepaid balance with a concurrency increase and no rate change.

## Kiro Pro+

Kiro Pro+ is USD 40 a user a month for 2,000 credits and add-on credits at USD 0.04
(`documented`, `kiro.dev/pricing/`, read 2026-10-22). Pro Max is USD 100 for 5,000
and Power USD 200 for 10,000.

The credits per dollar are flat across the ladder at twenty per dollar, so the only
reason to climb is the total. Against the USD 0.04 add-on rate, every tier carries
the same 50 per cent discount on top-ups.

Evidence: `kiro.dev/pricing/` (read 2026-10-22): 2,000 credits at USD 40. Verdict: a
linear ladder with a constant top-up discount.

## Cursor Premium

Cursor Premium is USD 30 a month, 4x the usage of Plus, with cloud agents,
automations and third-party models at API list price from prepaid credit
(`documented`, `cursor.com/docs/models-and-pricing`, read 2026-10-22).

Premium is the first tier that admits third-party models, and it admits them at cost.
That is a different offer from the one the legacy ladder made: legacy Pro, Pro+ and
Ultra included third-party models in an "Other Models" pool. On the new ladder a
Claude or GPT request is billed at the model's own list price out of prepaid credit,
so the plan's value is confined to Cursor's own models.

Evidence: `cursor.com/docs/models-and-pricing` (read 2026-10-22): Premium at USD 30
with 4x Plus and third-party models at list price. Verdict: the entry tier for
third-party models, at cost, with the pool size unstated.

## Z.ai Pro

The GLM Coding Plan's Pro tier carries 12,000 five-hour credits and 60,000 weekly
credits, six times Lite's 2,000 and 10,000 (`documented`,
`docs.z.ai/devpack/overview`, read 2026-10-22). The credit formula and the per-model
multipliers are the same as on Lite.

The price is the gap. The subscribe page renders Lite, Pro and Max as tier names with
no numbers, and two different Pro prices have been recorded, neither confirmed, so the
record carries the credit allowances and no price for Pro.

Evidence: `docs.z.ai/devpack/overview` (read 2026-10-22): the allowances and the
formula. Verdict: a published allowance with an unreadable price.

## Anthropic Max 5x

Claude Max is "From $100" a month with a choice of 5x or 20x the usage of Pro per
five-hour session, higher output limits, priority access and USD 100 to USD 200 a
month in API credits (`documented`, `anthropic.com/pricing`, read 2026-10-22).

The API credit is the part a reader can convert. USD 100 a month of Claude Platform
credit is a real, priced allowance against published per-token rates, and it is
worth roughly USD 100 by definition. The usage multiple is not convertible at all,
because the baseline it multiplies is unstated.

So Max is two products in one price: a known USD 100 to USD 200 of API credit, and an
unknown multiple of an unknown plan.

Evidence: `anthropic.com/pricing` (read 2026-10-22): Max from USD 100 with a 5x or
20x choice and USD 100 or USD 200 in API credits. Verdict: half a convertible
product at twice the price of the bracket below it.

## Kimi Plus

Kimi's current domestic ladder is Go at CNY 49, Plus at CNY 99, Pro at CNY 199, Max
at CNY 699 and Ultra at CNY 1,399 a month, with annual totals of CNY 468, 948, 1,908,
6,708 and 13,428 (`documented`, `kimi.com/help/membership/membership-pricing`, read
2026-10-22).

Go excludes Kimi Code, so Plus is the entry point for a coding workload. Plus and above
carry a rolling five-hour limit and no weekly limit on Kimi Code, while the older
tier names keep both limits. The shared monthly quota pool covers web and Kimi Code
together and does not carry unused quota into the next month.

No tier publishes a token or credit number, so the ladder can be read only as prices
in CNY. The USD ladder does not appear on this page at all; it is withdrawn in
`history/2026-10-22T032433Z/`.

Evidence: `kimi.com/help/membership/membership-pricing` (read 2026-10-22): the five
tiers and their CNY prices, the Go exclusion and the shared pool. Verdict: a real
ladder with a published price and no published capacity.

## What did not work

- **Cursor Pro and Pro+.** Legacy. A new account cannot buy either, so a comparison
  that includes them compares against a closed door.
- **Z.ai Pro at USD 72 or USD 80.** Two figures, neither readable today. Carried as a
  gap, not as a row.
- **Kimi's USD ladder.** Third-party sourced and inconsistent with the first-party
  CNY ladder. Withdrawn.
- **Cerebras Code.** Every paid tier was sold out in September, and no live tier was
  found in this pass.

## Chaining

The bracket's useful property is that two USD 40 plans are cheaper than one USD 100
plan and carry two independent quotas. OpenCode Go Plus for generation and Copilot
Pro+ for the editor gives two ceilings that fail separately, which matters because
one exhausted quota that blocks everything is the failure mode this whole topic is
about.
