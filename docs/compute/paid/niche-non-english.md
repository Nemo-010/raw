# The niche market: nine non-English sellers under USD 5

Nine machines, found by searching in the language of each market rather than in English.
The premise is that the popular providers have tightened signup to pay for free abuse while
the niche ones have not, and that an English-language search lands on the same twenty hosts
every listicle names.

**The result is a split verdict.** This market does not beat the catalogue on capacity per
dollar. It beats it on two things: **a written renewal price**, and **a signup that takes a
bank transfer or a domestic QR code instead of a card**.

⚠ **Every row here is `documented`, not `measured`.** No account was created and no machine
was booted. Each price and shape is a vendor page read on 2026-10-24.

## The row that matters: Bulutova

**Bulutova «VPS Sunucu 8», Istanbul — 8 GB ECC, 2 vCore, 60 GB NVMe, 1 Gbps, at 168.87 TRY
a month plus the 20% KDV the cart adds, USD 4.07.** It is the only 8 GiB shape found under
USD 5 in any of the nine languages.

It does **not** win on GiB per USD: 1.96 against the incumbent's 2.13. At the vendor's
KDV-exclusive 168.87 TRY it returns 2.36, so the comparison turns on a VAT line this page
does not adjudicate.

What it wins on is **durability of the claim**, which is the incumbent's weakness. The
current Q1 row is clearance stock with no published renewal, which makes its price a moment
rather than a price. Bulutova publishes a list price, so the downside is knowable: if the
40% discount lapses the renewal is **USD 6.79** and over the line, but it is written on the
page and a buyer can see it coming.

The seller is verifiable in a way most of this market is not: BİTWİSE YAZILIM İNTERNET VE
TİCARET LİMİTED ŞİRKETİ, in the Turkish government ETBİS registry since 25.04.2022, Gebze
trade register 33061, tax number 1781478560, Mersis 0178147856000001, and a Kocaeli address
matching its contact page.

⛔ **It takes Havale/EFT bank transfer**, so a buyer with no card and no institutional
affiliation can buy it. Eight GiB, a published renewal, a registry entry and no card exist
together in none of the other eight rows.

## Why 2.13 GiB per USD is hard to beat, measured

IHOR.ONLINE's Russian price list sells RAM at the margin: **+1 GB RAM = 100 RUB/month = USD
1.23/GiB**, read first-party. To reach 2.13 GiB per USD a seller must price RAM near USD
0.47/GiB, a third of the going marginal rate. The incumbent is cheap because it is clearance
stock, not because the market is cheap.

## Four markets price themselves out

That is the finding, not a gap.

- **Romania** starts at EUR 5: `2gb.ro` Pachet 1 is 2 GB / 1 vCPU / 40 GB / unlimited for
  EUR 5 a month = USD 5.60, over the line before VAT.
- **Poland** is worse: `doomhosting.pl` sells 4 GB at 24 PLN = USD 6.11 and 8 GB at 48 PLN =
  USD 12.23; OVHcloud's Polish VPS-1 at 20.07 PLN brutto is USD 5.11.
- **Brazil** is worse again: `homehost.com.br` at R$13.90 and `locaweb` at R$15.90 buy 1 GB;
  `cloudrun.com.br` wants R$42.90 for 2 GB = USD 8.51.
- **Vietnam's** 4 GB rung misses too: AZVPS VPS Cheap 4 at 157,300 VND = USD 6.09.

## The counterparty test, which most of this market fails

One provider in this market kept selling annual plans after its named UK company was
dissolved in 2023. So every row here was tested for a legal entity.

**Three of nine pass.** Bulutova, Netlen and LumaDock were checked against a register and all
three are trading. LumaDock needs a caveat of its own: LUMADOCK LTD, 15844051, is **Active**,
incorporated 17 July 2024, previously LIFEINCLOUD LTD until 27 March 2026 — so unlike the
dissolved provider it is not dead, but its site says "based in London W1S 1HN" while Companies
House gives a Harrow HA3 8DP registered office, a formation-agent address, and it runs
Romanian infrastructure on a +40 number. **A two-year-old renamed company is not a dead one;
it is a reason not to prepay a year.**

**Six of nine name no legal entity at all**: `vpsindonesia.co.id`, `kencang.com`,
`dockerhosting.ru`, `netx.hosting`, `azvps.vn` and the AZVPS seller print no company name,
registration number or address on the pages read, and WHOIS was not checked. None should be
prepaid annually on this evidence.

## What this page does not establish

- **Debit acceptance and card holds for six of nine.** Only Bulutova has payment evidence
  read first-party.
- **Whether any can listen on a port for WireGuard.** All are KVM VPS with a public IPv4 and
  no stated port block, but none was probed, so `allow_inbound_listen: true` in the records is
  an inference from the product class, not an observation.
- **No sub-USD machine was found here**, so the sub-dollar question gets nothing from this
  sweep. The
  cheapest shapes were Kencang K-1 at Rp29,900 for 1 GB (USD 1.65, first-party) and
  IHOR.ONLINE «Старт» at 768 MB, which a search snippet prices at 100 RUB (USD 1.23) but whose
  price did not render in the page source.
- **Chinese is the largest unclosed gap.** The leads are real — 量芯云 2核2G at ¥18/month
  (USD 2.70), HKGserver 4核4G at ¥29.9/month (USD 4.48), 莱卡云 4核4G at ¥368/year (USD
  4.59/mo) — but every one came from an aggregator (`vps233.com`, `pianyivps.com`,
  `befun.ink`) and no first-party Chinese vendor page was reached. A mainland node requires
  实名认证, a national-ID check a registrant without a PRC identity cannot satisfy, while Hong Kong and
  overseas nodes from the same sellers often need only an email and Alipay. **That distinction
  is the whole value of the Chinese market and it could not be verified, so no Chinese record
  exists rather than one with a guessed identity.**
- **One price discrepancy recorded rather than resolved.** A search index returned
  `dockerhosting.ru` «Старт» at 145 RUB for the same 1-core / 2 GB / 15 GB shape the live page
  prices at 299 RUB. The live page is the price of record; whether the index is stale or the
  price doubled is unknown.

## How these were found

Searched in the local language throughout: Romanian `VPS ieftin`, Ukrainian `VPS недорого` /
`хостинг VPS Україна`, Russian `аренда VPS` / `VPS 8 ГБ` / `VPS за 100 рублей`, Polish
`tani VPS`, Turkish `ucuz VPS` / `VPS sunucu`, Indonesian `VPS murah`, Brazilian Portuguese
`VPS barato`, Vietnamese `VPS giá rẻ`, Chinese `便宜 VPS` / `VPS 年付`.

LowEndBox, LowEndTalk and HostBalls all answered and are alive; LowEndBox's Russia and Brazil
tags are the obvious next route into this market and its offer list was not mined.
`serverhunter.com` was, and its index is where a Romanian USD 2.99 / 2 GB lead came from
before it went stale against the vendor.

⭐ **The lesson, stated as a rule for this topic.** Search in the language of the market before
writing either a positive or a negative. Every refuted negative in this corpus — "nothing
monthly under USD 1", "no always-free VM without a card" — came from an English-language
search over English-language hosts. The sub-dollar market is Chinese and Russian, and one
query in each language produced a counterexample.
